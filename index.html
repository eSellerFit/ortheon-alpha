Skills alone are not enough.

Many career tools look mainly at resumes, job titles, or keywords. But a serious career move depends on more than what you have done before.

You may be qualified and still misaligned.

A role can fit your skills but conflict with your values, energy, autonomy needs, or lifestyle.

A meaningful path may be financially unrealistic.

Some transitions require lower income, bridge work, retraining, or a longer runway.

Some roles are transforming quickly.

AI, automation, and digital tools are changing which roles grow, shrink, or require new capabilities.

Career change is often not one jump.

The right move may require a staged path, a transfer point, or a safer bridge before the destination.

The Ortheon Method

Ortheon is being designed as a structured career decision-support tool, not a generic job quiz.

1

Competence Fit

What you can credibly do based on your experience, roles, industries, and transferable skills.

2

Career Anchors

What gives your work energy, dignity, motivation, and long-term fit.

3

Financial Reality

Whether a path can support your income needs, transition runway, and real-life obligations.

4

Decision Archetype

Whether your current decision is driven more by competence, meaning, money, autonomy, stability, or reinvention.

5

AI & Digital Role Durability

Whether the roles you are considering are likely to grow, transform, become automated, or lose value as AI and digital tools reshape work.

6

Transition Route

Whether your next direction is a direct move, a staged path, or a transition that requires a bridge first.

Who this is for

Ortheon is designed for people who need more than generic career advice.

Mid-career professionals

People considering a serious shift after years of accumulated experience.

Career transitioners

People choosing between corporate work, consulting, entrepreneurship, retraining, or mission-driven paths.

People balancing meaning and money

People who want a better fit but cannot ignore income, family, or financial obligations.

Career coaches and advisors

Professionals interested in structured pre-session diagnostics and decision-support tools.
