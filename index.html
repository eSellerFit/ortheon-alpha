The Ortheon Method

Ortheon is being designed as a structured career decision-support tool, not a generic job quiz.

1

Competence Fit

What you can credibly do based on your experience, roles, industries, and transferable skills.

2

Career Anchors

What gives your work energy, dignity, motivation, and long-term fit.

3

Financial Reality

Whether a path can support your income needs, transition runway, and real-life obligations.

4

Decision Archetype

How you personally weight the trade-offs — between meaning, income, autonomy, stability, and growth.

5

AI & Digital Role Durability

Whether the roles you are considering are likely to grow, transform, become automated, or lose value.

6

Transition Route

Whether your next direction is a direct move, a staged path, or a transition that requires a bridge first.

Sample Career Direction Map

Ortheon will visualize possible role-to-role transitions from your current position. Nodes represent roles. Lines represent career directions. Line length reflects estimated transition horizon.
