## 1. Candidate Summary

Polina Serebryakova is a senior enterprise HR / People professional with more than 20 years of HR experience, including long-tenured Citigroup work across international and regulated banking contexts.

The CV shows senior HR advisor and director-level scope, including HR service delivery for US Personal Banking Operations, HR advisory to senior leadership, HR coverage for Global Risk Management segments, MEA HR risk and control initiatives, HR strategic projects, cluster HR leadership across Israel, Kazakhstan, and Russia, employee relations, change management, senior-leader coaching, recruitment / employee relations centers of excellence, and HR function delivery across multiple countries.

The existing Ortheon report identifies the main pattern as "HR, talent systems, workforce intelligence, and people operations" and recommends:

- Talent Acquisition / People Operations - Enterprise
- Workforce Planning / Talent Intelligence - Enterprise
- People Analytics / HR Tech - Enterprise

For library validation, this is a strong test of the People & Organization spine, Workforce Intelligence spine, mission-adjacent workforce development paths, independent advisory paths, and false-positive suppression for marketplace, product, marketing, founder, and generic operations paths.

## 2. Strong Evidence Signals

- HR leadership: director-level HR advisory and HR function delivery across large, regulated enterprise contexts.
- HRBP: direct HR advisor scope to business heads, senior leadership teams, risk groups, and regional business initiatives.
- Talent / people strategy: people operations, workforce implications of business strategy, employee relations, benefits, severance, organizational change, and talent-system signals.
- Organizational development / change: implementation of organizational changes, post-reorganization sustainability framework, senior leader coaching, and change management across employee populations.
- Learning / development: evidence of a continuous learning project ("Habit to Learn") across MEA HR and HRSS; this is relevant but not enough by itself to treat L&D leadership as the primary direction.
- Business partnership: strong support for business units and senior stakeholders inside Citigroup.
- People operations: HR service delivery, centers of excellence, employee relations, benefits, and HR process ownership.
- Advisory potential: senior HR domain expertise, cross-country scope, coaching, change work, and executive stakeholder exposure create credible future advisory positioning, though independent-practice evidence still needs validation.

## 3. Weak Or Noisy Signals

- Citigroup / banking / finance context should not be over-weighted into finance, risk, compliance, or generic business operations unless the work is clearly outside HR.
- Risk, control, audit, and regulatory vocabulary appears in an HR context; it should not automatically route to RC-01, RC-02, FC, or broad operations families.
- "Operations" appears in US Personal Banking Operations, but the candidate's role is HR advisory to that business, not native business operations leadership.
- People analytics / HR tech should be treated as bridge or modifier evidence unless the CV shows direct people analytics function ownership, HRIS ownership, data product ownership, or technical HR systems leadership.
- Seniority should not inflate every adjacent path. Director-level HR experience does not make the candidate a director-level product manager, marketplace operator, transformation consultant, or independent advisor without bridge evidence.
- Marketplace / platform vocabulary in the report appears unsupported by the visible CV evidence and should not surface as a nearby direction for this profile.
- Founder / builder paths should suppress unless there is explicit founder, venture, marketplace, product, commercial, or runway evidence.

## 9. Report-Quality Findings

Should HR Director / HR VP / People Leadership appear?

Yes. The strongest canonical family should likely be PO-01 - People / HR Leadership, with title examples such as HR Director, VP People, Head of People, or People Operations Director. The current report's "Talent Acquisition / People Operations - Enterprise" label partially captures this, but it blends multiple families and may understate general HR leadership.

Should HRBP appear?

Yes. PO-02 - HR Business Partnership should appear clearly because the CV contains strong embedded HR advisor and senior business-partner evidence. If HRBP is missing from the output, that is likely an implementation / scoring issue or report presentation issue, not a library taxonomy issue.

Should L&D / Workforce Development appear as adjacent or bridge?

Yes, but with discipline. PO-05 should appear as adjacent or bridge-based if the "Habit to Learn" project and coaching signals are confirmed as program ownership. MP-05 should be conditional or bridge-based, not primary, unless future evidence shows adult learning, workforce development, career services, employer partnerships, or cohort outcomes.

Should Marketplace Operations suppress?

Yes. Marketplace / Platform Operations should suppress for this profile. The existing report's nearby "Marketplace / Platform Operations - Enterprise" trajectory appears unsupported by the visible evidence. That is an implementation / scoring issue, not a library issue, because FB-03 already has strict evidence gates requiring marketplace/platform building, network-effects understanding, and supply/demand operating work.

Other report-quality observations:

- "Talent Acquisition / People Operations - Enterprise" should map back to canonical families rather than remain a composite direction. It appears to combine PO-01, PO-02, and PO-03.
- "Workforce Planning / Talent Intelligence - Enterprise" should be split or mapped to WI-01 and possibly WI-04. In this profile, WI-01 looks bridge-based unless explicit workforce planning ownership is found.
- "People Analytics / HR Tech - Enterprise" should be mapped to WI-02 and/or WI-03, not treated as a generic D3 enterprise direction without the specific evidence gates.
- Financial constraints are correctly visible: the report shows a $10,000 monthly floor, 6-month runway, and first-year estimates below or near that floor. Bridge and independent paths should be cautious.
- The report's "Credible now" badge on bridge directions may be confusing. Bridge paths can be credible to validate, but they should not read as fully direct.

## 10. Library Adjustment Recommendations

No immediate taxonomy changes recommended.

The library already contains the necessary boundaries:

- PO-01 separates broad People / HR Leadership from HRBP, TA, OD, and L&D.
- PO-02 has strong embedded HRBP gates.
- PO-03 prevents generic HR/recruiting exposure from becoming TA leadership.
- PO-04 prevents culture or change participation from becoming OD leadership.
- PO-05 prevents training delivery or single projects from becoming L&D leadership.
- WI-01 prevents generic strategic HR or dashboards from becoming workforce planning.
- MP-05 prevents mentoring / career interest from becoming workforce development leadership.
- IP-01 requires pipeline, runway, and business-development evidence.
- FB-03 already suppresses non-marketplace profiles.

Likely issues from this pressure test:

- Implementation / scoring issue, not library issue: Marketplace / Platform Operations surfaced as a nearby trajectory despite absent marketplace evidence.
- Implementation / scoring issue, not library issue: HRBP may be under-explicit if the report does not show PO-02 or an equivalent HRBP direction.
- Report presentation issue, not library issue: composite labels such as "Talent Acquisition / People Operations" and "People Analytics / HR Tech" obscure which canonical families were actually matched.
- Report presentation issue, not library issue: bridge directions labeled "Credible now" may overstate readiness unless direct versus bridge-based status is visually and textually clearer.

Recommended cleanup before implementation:

1. Preserve PO-01 and PO-02 as the primary senior HR / People routes for this profile.
2. Keep PO-03, PO-04, PO-05, WI-01, MP-05, IP-01, and SA-04 as adjacent, conditional, or bridge-based depending on evidence.
3. Add a pressure-test assertion that marketplace/platform paths must suppress unless marketplace/platform evidence is explicit.
4. Require canonical family IDs in future report QA so composite labels can be audited against the library.
5. Re-run this profile after report logic can separate Direct, Adjacent, Bridge-based, Conditional, and Suppressed outcomes.

## Pressure Test #1 Verdict

Library taxonomy issue?

No material taxonomy issue found. The 69-family library has the right families and the right evidence gates for a senior HR / People profile.

Engine/scoring issue?

Likely yes. Marketplace / Platform Operations appearing as a nearby trajectory is a false positive against the library gates. HRBP may also need stronger surfacing if the current report output does not explicitly expose it.

Report presentation issue?

Yes. The report should show canonical family mapping and avoid broad composite direction labels that blend PO-01, PO-02, PO-03, WI-01, WI-02, and WI-03. Bridge paths should be labeled so they do not read like direct current readiness.

Recommended next action.

Run the first three pressure-test profiles manually before creating any implementation spec. For this profile specifically, use the validated manual mapping to calibrate PO-01 / PO-02 surfacing, keep WI and L&D paths as evidence-bounded bridges, and suppress marketplace, product, marketing, founder, customer operations, and generic business operations paths unless new source evidence appears.
