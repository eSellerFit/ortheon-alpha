## 1. Candidate Summary

George Chakhidze is a broad hybrid workforce, marketplace, operations, advisory, and founder/operator profile.

The profile is centered on workforce planning, labor operations, demand forecasting, capacity modeling, frontline / contingent workforce systems, talent acquisition operations, labor marketplace mechanics, and advisory work. The strongest evidence comes from large-scale workforce planning and labor marketplace environments: Wildberries, QUGO Tech, Sberbank, Kaspersky, Abbott, PepsiCo, Kelly OCG / Exect Partners / Molten Consulting, GHUB Consulting, and SCORE Greater New York.

The profile is broad, but not merely vague-broad. It includes unusually specific evidence: 300K+ frontline worker capacity planning at Wildberries, 100K+ contractor marketplace scale at QUGO, 43,000 annual hires at Sberbank, predictive staffing models, AI-enabled recruiting tools, workforce-to-hiring conversion logic, labor cost modeling, seller/order-volume demand signals, marketplace supply acquisition, and founder/CEO responsibility in a gig workforce marketplace.

Pressure Test #2 should therefore not suppress marketplace/platform paths by default. Unlike Pressure Test #1, this profile has real marketplace/platform operating evidence. The test question is whether the library can surface the right direct families while preventing the profile from becoming "credible for everything."

## 3. Weak Or Noisy Signals

- Partial exposure vs. true ownership: working with Product, Analytics, or Finance should not automatically create Product Management, Data Science, or FP&A leadership recommendations.
- Founder interest vs. proven founder/operator evidence: QUGO is real founder/operator evidence, but new founder recommendations still need thesis, runway, market, team, and economics. Prior founder experience is not a blank check.
- Marketplace participation vs. marketplace building: Wildberries is marketplace operating context; QUGO is stronger marketplace-building evidence. Future engines should distinguish those.
- Operations involvement vs. operations leadership: capacity planning and labor operations are strong, but OD-01 should require cross-functional business operations ownership, not just workforce operations expertise.
- AI/tooling interest vs. substantive AI/digital evidence: this profile has substantive AI-enabled recruiting/workforce tooling, but that still should be treated inside workforce, TA, marketplace, and operations families unless the evidence supports a standalone AI/data/product family.
- Advisory signals vs. consulting-firm ladder: GHUB and SCORE support advisory and specialist consulting potential, but do not automatically equal MBB / Big 4-style SA-02 senior consulting-track equivalence.
- Seller ecosystem vocabulary: seller activation forecasts and marketplace operations should not automatically create enterprise sales, marketing/growth, or partnerships leadership recommendations unless revenue/account ownership is explicit.

## 9. False-Positive Risk

The biggest future-engine risk is that this profile is genuinely broad and successful, so shallow matching could surface too many senior directions.

High-risk false positives:

- Product Management: could surface because of Product partnership, marketplace systems, and algorithmic models. This should suppress or be bridge-only unless roadmap/product ownership is explicit.
- Engineering Leadership: could surface because of AI, algorithms, and systems language. Suppress; technical authority and engineering team leadership are absent.
- Marketing / Growth Leadership: could surface because of marketplace scaling, contractor acquisition, and 32x revenue growth. Suppress unless the evidence shows owned marketing/growth function.
- Enterprise Sales Leadership: could surface because of revenue scaling and client work. Suppress without quota, sales team, and deal ownership.
- Business Operations Leadership: should surface only as adjacent/conditional. Do not over-level from workforce operations into generic COO.
- Management Consulting: should surface, but senior firm-track equivalence should reset. GHUB/SCORE/advisory work is not automatically Partner-track consulting.
- Boutique Consulting Practice: should be bridge-based unless there is team, firm structure, and multi-client revenue model.
- Financial Advisory: unit economics and financial scenario modeling are business-advisory tools, not FC-02 evidence.
- Licensed paths: must suppress due credential/domain gates.
- Marketplace / Platform Venture Builder: should surface for this profile, but only as workforce marketplace/platform builder. A generic marketplace founder recommendation should require current venture thesis, team, runway, and risk tolerance.

## 10. Library Adjustment Recommendations

No immediate library taxonomy changes recommended.

The library already has useful boundaries:

- PO-03 captures true TA leadership rather than generic recruiting.
- WI-01 captures workforce planning and talent strategy with strong analytical and planning gates.
- WI-04 captures talent intelligence separately from recruiting execution.
- OD-01 requires broad cross-functional business operations ownership, preventing single-function operations inflation.
- SA-02 distinguishes consulting-firm work from internal/advisory work.
- SA-04 captures transformation advisory without collapsing it into generic consulting.
- IP-01 and IP-02 separate solo advisory from boutique firm-building.
- FB-03 already distinguishes marketplace/platform building from marketplace participation.
- MP-05 prevents corporate workforce interest from becoming mission-sector workforce development without program/outcome evidence.

Likely issues to watch:

- Implementation / scoring issue, not library issue: broad profile may over-surface weak product, engineering, marketing, enterprise sales, or generic operations directions.
- Implementation / scoring issue, not library issue: engine may fail to distinguish QUGO-level marketplace building from Wildberries marketplace participation/context.
- Report presentation issue, not library issue: report should expose canonical IDs and path classifications so FB-03, WI-01, PO-03, OD-01, IP-01, and SA-04 do not collapse into one vague "workforce marketplace operator" label.
- Report presentation issue, not library issue: direct, adjacent, bridge-based, and conditional paths must be visibly separated because this profile has strong evidence in some families and tempting but weak evidence in others.

Recommended QA rules from this test:

1. Marketplace/platform recommendations require evidence type tagging: participation, operating context, operating ownership, or founder/building ownership.
2. Broad hybrid profiles should receive seniority preservation only inside evidence-native families.
3. Product, engineering, marketing, and sales paths should require their own family gates, not adjacent founder/operator vocabulary.
4. Advisory paths should separate IP-01 solo practice, IP-02 boutique firm, SA-02 consulting-firm career, and SA-04 transformation advisory.
5. Report output should show underlying canonical family ID for every displayed recommendation.

## Pressure Test #2 Verdict

Library taxonomy issue?

No material taxonomy issue found. The 69-family library can handle this broad hybrid profile if its evidence gates are honored. It has the right distinctions among PO-03, WI-01, WI-04, OD-01, SA-02, SA-04, IP-01, IP-02, FB-01, FB-03, and MP-05.

Engine/scoring issue?

Likely high risk. A future engine may over-recommend product, engineering, marketing, sales, generic operations, or consulting paths because the profile has broad seniority, marketplace vocabulary, AI/tooling language, and founder/operator experience. Scoring must privilege family-specific ownership over keyword adjacency.

Report presentation issue?

Likely high risk. This profile needs clear canonical family IDs, path types, level resets, and evidence explanations. Composite labels would be especially dangerous because the candidate has real evidence across several adjacent families.

Recommended next action.

Before implementation, add QA expectations for broad hybrid profiles: preserve direct recommendations for WI-01, PO-03, and workforce-marketplace FB-03 where evidence is strong; classify OD-01, SA-02, SA-04, IP-01, IP-02, FB-01, WI-04, and MP-05 with explicit path type and level reset; suppress product, engineering, marketing, enterprise sales, financial advisory, and licensed paths unless their independent evidence gates are met.
